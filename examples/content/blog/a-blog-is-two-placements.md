# A blog is two placements

A blog needs no database, no identity and no API. The posts are fixed
when the site is built, so they are `static`; the reader's filter box is
theirs and is gone on reload, so it is `client`.

That is the whole application. Every gap involving remote execution,
persistence or authentication is out of scope for it, which is why a blog
is the first real program this language can finish.
